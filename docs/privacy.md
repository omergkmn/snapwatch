# Snapwatch – Privacy Policy

Snapwatch is a demo app built for a Flutter developer study case.

- **Account:** you use the app as a guest. Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous user ID; no name, e-mail or phone number is sent to it.
- **On your device:** your name, favourite genres, watchlist and plan choice are stored only on your device and are removed when you reset the app or uninstall it.
- **Screenshots:** an image you choose to scan is sent to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic to recognise the film or series. It is not stored by Snapwatch.
- **No tracking:** the app has no analytics or advertising SDKs.
